Commission rates (on amount paid, excluding SST)

Three rules for every HOD: everyone's commission on a sale together stays within the cap (margin × 25%, or × 40% for partner-delivered services); prices and discounts are set by the company; rates change once a quarter, never mid-month. Refunded sales take the commission back in the month of the refund.
